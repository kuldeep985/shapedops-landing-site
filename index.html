02How an order moves

Watch one order go from enquiry to paid.

This is the manufacturing stage template ShapedOps starts from. Scroll to move the order — every document and WhatsApp message on the right is generated by the system, not typed by your team.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    A buyer asks for a rate. Log it in seconds — or bring it in with the rest of your Excel sheet. It gets an owner and a place on the board.

    Owner assignedOn the board
  2. 02

    Quote sent

    Move it to Quote sent and the quotation PDF is generated on your letterhead, with your terms. The client gets a WhatsApp update.

    Quotation PDFWhatsApp update
  3. 03

    PO received

    The PO comes in. The agreement is generated, and the client gets an order confirmation on WhatsApp — before anyone remembers to send one.

    Agreement PDFOrder confirmation
  4. 04

    In production

    The shop floor works from the same record. The client has a tracking link, so “where's my order?” answers itself.

    Tracking linkWhatsApp update
  5. 05

    QC & dispatch

    Inspection done, truck booked. The dispatch update goes out the moment the stage changes — nobody has to type it.

    WhatsApp update
  6. 06

    Invoiced

    The invoice is generated from the order — no retyping rates or quantities — with an online payment link through your gateway.

    Invoice PDFPayment link
  7. 07

    Payment collected

    Payment lands, the receipt is issued and the order closes. If it doesn't land, reminders go out at 30, 60 and 90 days overdue.

    Receipt PDF30 / 60 / 90-day reminders

09Questions

Questions owners ask us.

Something not covered here? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

We run everything on Excel, registers and WhatsApp today. Is that a problem?
No — that's exactly who ShapedOps is built for. During setup we import the Excel sheets you already keep and shape the stages around how your orders move today, so your team doesn't have to change how it works first.
Is ShapedOps a CRM?
It covers what a CRM does — enquiries, follow-ups and a pipeline — but it's built around the order: production, dispatch, documents, invoices and payment collection. Think of it as an order-to-cash system shaped to your stages.
Do our clients need to install anything?
No. Updates reach them on WhatsApp, and their order tracking link opens in any browser — no app and no login.
Will documents carry our letterhead?
Yes. Your logo, company details and colours are set up once, and every quotation, agreement, invoice and receipt is generated with them.
Which WhatsApp and payment providers do you support?
We connect the WhatsApp Business provider and payment gateway you already use as part of implementation. Every account also gets WhatsApp click-to-chat links out of the box.
How is ShapedOps priced?
As an implementation, not a per-seat subscription: a one-time implementation fee plus a monthly maintenance fee, scoped to your locations, team and modules. You get a written proposal within 2–3 business days of a discovery call.
Do you work with businesses outside Gujarat?
Yes. We're based in Ahmedabad, and ShapedOps works for manufacturing and trading businesses anywhere in India.
Can we take our data out?
Yes. Owners can export their data from settings at any time.
