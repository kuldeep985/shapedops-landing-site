01The schedule

One reminder per milestone. Never a daily nag.

Reminders are based on how many days an invoice is overdue. Each invoice gets at most one reminder at 30, 60 and 90 days — firm, polite, and on time.

04Questions

Questions about reminders.

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

Are payment reminders on by default?
No. An owner turns them on. When they do, a default reminder template is created so reminders work straight away, and you can reword it at any time.
How often does a client get reminded?
At most once per milestone: when an invoice reaches 30, 60 and 90 days overdue. An invoice already reminded at its current milestone is skipped until it crosses the next one.
How do clients pay?
Invoices carry a Razorpay payment link, and the invoice is also available on the client's order tracking link.
Do we need a WhatsApp Business provider for reminders?
No. With a provider connected, reminders send automatically. Without one, each due reminder appears as a to-do with the message already written, ready to send with one tap.
Does ShapedOps replace our accounting software?
No. It tracks each order through to payment collection and issues the client-facing invoice and receipt. Your books can stay where they are, and owners can export data at any time.
