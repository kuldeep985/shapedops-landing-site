    01On the shop floor

    How orders are tracked today, and with ShapedOps

    The job card knows where the order is. The register knows the quantity. Excel knows the rate. The buyer knows nothing — so they call.

    When…TodayWith ShapedOps
    A buyer asks where the order is Someone calls the floor, then calls the buyer back. They open their tracking link: stage, documents, invoice.
    You quote a rate Typed into Word, saved as quote_final_2.docx. Move the order to Quote sent — the quotation PDF is generated on your letterhead.
    The PO comes in Sits in someone's inbox, maybe printed. Recorded against the order; the agreement is generated and the buyer is updated.
    Material is dispatched Confirmed in a WhatsApp group of fourteen people. QC & dispatch is a stage. Moving it sends the buyer a WhatsApp update.
    A payment is late Someone remembers — or doesn't. Invoice with a pay-online link, and reminders at 30, 60 and 90 days overdue.
    It's month-end The numbers are rebuilt from the register. Pipeline value, closed revenue and conversion, already calculated from the orders.

    05Questions

    What manufacturers ask us.

    Something else? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

    Can ShapedOps track heat numbers, material grades or drawing revisions?
    Yes. During setup we add custom fields for whatever your shop tracks — heat number, grade, drawing revision, pattern number, transporter — and they sit on the order record next to quantity, rate and stage.
    Which documents does ShapedOps generate?
    Quotations, agreements, invoices, payment receipts and completion certificates, on your letterhead, each at the stage you choose. Dispatch is tracked as a stage on the order: when it moves, your buyer gets a WhatsApp update.
    Do we have to replace our accounting software?
    No. ShapedOps isn't an accounting package. It tracks the order from enquiry to payment collection and issues the client-facing documents; your books can stay where they are, and owners can export data at any time.
    We run orders on Excel, a register and WhatsApp groups. Can we still start?
    Yes — that's where most manufacturers start. We import the sheets you already keep, flag possible duplicates before anything is saved, and set the stages up around how your orders move today.
    Can our buyers check order status themselves?
    Yes. Every order has a tracking link that opens in any browser, with the current stage, documents to download and invoices to pay. No app and no login.
    Do you work with manufacturers outside India?
    Yes. ShapedOps is built in Ahmedabad, India, and works for manufacturing and trading businesses anywhere in the world — setup, training and support all happen online.
