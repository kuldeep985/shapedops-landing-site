Upload

04Questions

Questions about moving off Excel.

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

Which file types can we import?
Excel (.xlsx and .xls) and CSV files. Both orders and contacts can be imported.
What happens to duplicate rows?
Rows with the same email or phone number — within the file or as a record already in ShapedOps — are flagged in the preview and skipped on import, so you start clean.
Do our column names have to match?
No. Column headers are matched to fields automatically where they line up, and you can change any match before previewing.
Can we still use Excel for anything?
Yes. ShapedOps isn't an accounting package, so your books can stay where they are, and owners can export data whenever they need it.
Who does the import?
We do it with you during implementation, along with setting up your stages, letterhead and custom fields.
