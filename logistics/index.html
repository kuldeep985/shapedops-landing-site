04Questions

What logistics teams ask us.

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

Are the logistics stages fixed?
No. The seven stages are where setup starts. We rename them, add or remove steps, and choose which ones issue documents so the board matches how your desk actually works.
Can shippers check a booking without logging in?
Yes. Every booking has a tracking link that opens in any browser and shows the current stage, the documents issued so far and the invoice to pay. No app and no login.
Does ShapedOps file e-way bills or customs documents?
No. ShapedOps tracks each booking's stage, issues the client-facing quotation, agreement, invoice and receipt, and keeps your shipper updated. Filings stay in the systems you use for them today.
How do we collect payment?
The invoice carries a Razorpay payment link. If it stays unpaid, the shipper gets one reminder each at 30, 60 and 90 days overdue, and the receipt is issued when the payment is collected.
Can we bring in bookings we already track in Excel?
Yes. Upload an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file, match its columns to fields, and preview the import. Rows with the same email or phone as an existing record are flagged and skipped.
