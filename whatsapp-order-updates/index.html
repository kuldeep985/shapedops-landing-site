02Two ways to send

Automatic, or one tap. Your choice.

Start with click-to-chat on day one, and switch to automatic sending whenever you connect a WhatsApp Business provider.

Automatic

Sent the moment an order moves

With a WhatsApp Business provider connected during setup, messages go out from your business number as soon as the stage changes — including overdue reminders.

Click-to-chat

Written for you, sent with one tap

No provider account needed. ShapedOps prepares the message; you open it in WhatsApp and press send. Overdue reminders appear as to-dos so none are missed.

Tracking link

The answer to "where is my order?"

Each order's link shows its stage, documents and invoice in any browser. No app, no login. See the client portal →

04Questions

Questions about WhatsApp updates.

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90998 67128.

Do we need the WhatsApp Business API?
No. Every account can use click-to-chat: the message is written for you and sent with one tap from WhatsApp. If you want messages to go out automatically, we connect a WhatsApp Business provider during setup.
Which WhatsApp providers can you connect?
Visity is supported out of the box, and most other REST-based WhatsApp Business providers can be connected through configuration during implementation.
Can we change what the messages say?
Yes. Each message comes from a template you can edit. Placeholders such as the client's name, the stage and the invoice amount fill in from the order.
Will the messages come from our number?
With a provider connected, messages go out from your WhatsApp Business number. With click-to-chat, they're sent from whichever WhatsApp you tap send in.
Does our buyer need to install anything?
No. Updates arrive in their normal WhatsApp, and their tracking link opens in any browser without a login.
